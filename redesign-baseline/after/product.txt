SETUP AND CARE

A 14' × 15' frame needs 17 sticks of 1" EMT in 10' lengths. Order angles only and buy EMT locally to save freight.

Let your tent dry for about 48–72 hours before storage. Clean with mild soap and a soft brush. A hard container is recommended for storage.

ANATOMY OF A DAVIS WALL TENT
01
5' walls (cut size)

Walls are cut at five feet and will finish at 4' 8".

Tall walls give usable floor space right to the edge for cots and gear.

02
Zippered door

Heavy-duty #10 YKK coil zipper.

A screen door receiver is standard, so a screen door can be added at any time.

03
5.5" stove jack

Standard location is inside left as you enter. Two layers of fiberglass cloth. 4", 5" or 6" available.

The jack can be moved to another location for no additional charge.

04
Attached sod cloth

A 10-inch band sewn in around the inside of the tent at the bottom.

Keeps drafts, snow and critters out where the wall meets the ground.

05
Ridge and eave sleeves

Accept a 1" pipe or a 12" log.

Pitch on a frame or cut poles on site.

06
Pre-cut ropes

Steel stamped tensioners. No knots required.

Ropes arrive cut to length so the tent goes up quickly.

07
Steel stakes

12-inch for ropes and 10-inch for the tent bottom, in their own bag.

Steel holds in frozen ground where plastic fails.
