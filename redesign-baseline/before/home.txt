THE CANVAS

The fabric is the argument

We only use cotton canvas to make our tents.

10 oz. double-fill cotton army duck

Uncombed, long-fiber canvas. Its tear and burst strength is nearly double that of comparable fabrics.

Sunforger® treatment is only applied in the United States.

The Sunforger® treatment is applied to our canvas in Butler, GA.

Highly resistant to moisture, mildew and UV damage.

With proper care, our canvas will easily last our average user a lifetime.
